\chapter{Checklist Before You Publish}\label{app:checklist}

Use this list to turn the template into your own book.

\begin{itemize}[label=$\square$]
  \item Replace the title, subtitle, author, date and publisher in
        \file{main.tex}.
  \item Write your own copyright and license statement on the back of the
        title page and in the \file{LICENSE} file.
  \item Delete or rewrite the preface in \file{frontmatter/}.
  \item Remove the sample chapters and appendices from \file{chapters/} and
        \file{appendices/}, and from the \cmd{include} lines in \file{main.tex}.
  \item Delete the sample figure and replace the entries of
        \file{references.bib}.
  \item Choose your colours, fonts and margins in the style file.
  \item Compile from scratch and check that the log contains no warnings about
        undefined references or citations.
  \item Read the PDF once from cover to cover, and check the table of
        contents, the hyperlinks and the page breaks.
\end{itemize}
