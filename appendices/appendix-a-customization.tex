\chapter{Customization Reference}\label{app:customization}

\Cref{tab:customization} lists the settings you are most likely to change and
where to find them. Options marked \emph{main} are in \file{main.tex};
options marked \emph{style} are in \file{style/booktemplate.sty}.

\section{Changing the Page Format}

The template is designed for A4 landscape. To use US Letter, replace
\texttt{paper=a4} by \texttt{paper=letter} in \file{main.tex}. Portrait
orientation is not supported by the default margins: remove
\texttt{paper=landscape} and adjust the values of \pkg{geometry} in the style
file.

\section{Changing the Language}

The language is set in Section~2 of the style file. Replace \texttt{english}
by another \pkg{babel} language, for example \texttt{spanish}, and translate
the captions of the callout boxes and the word \enquote{References}.
